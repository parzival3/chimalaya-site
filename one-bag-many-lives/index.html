Bag4Life

One bag, many different lives

Every newborn we visit at home receives a Bag4Life bag. The bag is the same each time; the lives it arrives in are not.

Inside the Bag4Life are natural, ecological, handmade Nepali products, made with care by local hands to support new mothers in those first tender weeks.

Choosing local, handmade products is more than a healthy choice: it is Chimalaya’s commitment to make every donation work twice. Our funds serve our cause, and they also sustain the small family businesses, artisans and communities around Kathmandu.
